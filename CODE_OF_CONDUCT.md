# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a respectful, inclusive, and welcoming environment for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Welcome different viewpoints and experience levels.
- Give and receive feedback professionally.
- Focus on what is best for the project and community.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insults, or disruptive behavior.
- Sharing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, issues, pull requests, or contributors that violate this code of conduct.
